Privacy Policy

Your privacy and security are important to us. This explains how Ghana Connect protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, Ghana Connect may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, profile details, photos, location preferences and verification information.

2. Ghana Card Verification

To maintain a trusted community, Ghana Connect is designed to support identity verification. Ghana Card information will only be used for account verification and security purposes.

  • Verification helps prevent fake accounts.
  • Personal identity information will not be publicly displayed.
  • Sensitive information will be protected.

3. How We Protect Your Data

Account Security

Passwords and account access are protected using security systems.

Private Information

Your personal details are controlled by privacy settings.

Secure Storage

User information will be stored using protected databases.

4. Payments

Premium subscriptions, profile boosts and advertising payments will use secure payment providers. Ghana Connect will not store your full bank card details.

5. User Control

Users can update their profile information, manage privacy settings, report accounts and request account support.

6. Future International Expansion

As Ghana Connect expands to Ghanaian communities in the UK, USA, Canada, Germany and other countries, additional privacy requirements and international data protection standards will be considered.
