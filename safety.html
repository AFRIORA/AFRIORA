Safety Center

Your safety is our priority. Learn how to stay protected while connecting with people.

Verified Members

Ghana Connect is designed to support identity verification to reduce fake accounts and improve trust.

Protect Your Information

Never share passwords, Ghana Card details, banking information or private documents with other users.

Avoid Scams

Be careful with users requesting money, emergency payments or suspicious offers.

Report Users

Report suspicious accounts, harassment, fake profiles or unsafe behavior.

Meeting Someone Safely

  • Take time to know someone before trusting them.
  • Use Ghana Connect messaging before sharing personal contacts.
  • Meet in public places when meeting offline.
  • Tell a trusted person before meeting someone new.
  • Trust your feelings and leave unsafe situations.

Community Rules

  • Respect other members.
  • No fake identities.
  • No harassment or abusive messages.
  • No fraud, scams or illegal activities.
  • Use the report system when necessary.

Important Safety Notice

Ghana Connect helps people connect, but users should always make careful decisions when communicating online or meeting someone in person.
